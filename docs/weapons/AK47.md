# The AK-47 — 7.62 × 39 mm assault rifle

Kalashnikov's rifle: a long-stroke gas piston, a rotating bolt, a curved 30-round magazine, wooden furniture. Fully
automatic. The model is our own (`scripts/oth/weapons.py`).

## The real machine

| | |
|---|---|
| cartridge | 7.62 × 39 mm M43, 7.9 g (122 gr) |
| muzzle velocity | 715 m/s |
| muzzle energy | ½ · 0.0079 · 715² = **2019 J** |
| momentum | 0.0079 · 715 = 5.65 kg·m/s |
| rate of fire | **600 rounds a minute** (cyclic) |
| magazine | 30 |
| mass | 3.47 kg empty, 4.3 kg loaded |
| length / barrel | 880 mm / 415 mm |
| useful range | 350 m |

## The tick

600 a minute is one round every 100 ms — **exactly 2 ticks** of the server's 20 Hz. So the rifle fires at its real
rate, and every shot falls on a tick: the trigger held, a round leaves on every second tick.

## The flight (k = 0.00136 /m)

| range | speed | time | ticks | drop | energy kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 m | 668 m/s | 0.072 s | 1.4 | 2.6 cm | 87 % |
| 100 m | 624 m/s | 0.150 s | 3.0 | 11 cm | 76 % |
| 200 m | 545 m/s | 0.321 s | 6.4 | 51 cm | 58 % |
| 300 m | 475 m/s | 0.518 s | 10.4 | 1.32 m | 44 % |
| 400 m | 415 m/s | 0.743 s | 14.9 | 2.71 m | 34 % |

Sighted at 100 m. Beyond 400 m the bullet is gone.

## The recoil

Free recoil: 5.65 kg·m/s (+ about a third again from the powder gas) ÷ 4.3 kg ≈ **1.7 m/s** a shot — and ten of them
a second. The muzzle climbs and wanders: in the game each shot kicks the view up 0.9° and ± 0.4° sideways; the kick
grows over the first 8 shots of a burst (to × 1.8), and eases back over 6 ticks once the trigger is let go. Short
bursts stay on a man at 100 m; a held trigger does not.

## In the game

- **Automatic: one round every 2 ticks (100 ms — 600 a minute)** while the trigger is held.
- **It shoots where the cross is**: the bullet leaves for what the middle of the screen is on — the burst climbs
  because the VIEW does (the kick above), not because the rounds leave the cross. **Spread** (the cross's gap opens
  to show it): 0.12° standing still, 0.4° walking, 1.1° running or in the air; + 0.07° a shot in a burst, to + 0.7°
  at most (settles in 8 ticks).
- **Pace** with it in the hands: 86 % (CS:GO's 215 of 250).
- **Damage 34** at the muzzle (three to the body), × 3.0 to the head (one), × 0.75 to a limb; falls with the energy
  kept, never under 50 %.
- **Reload 50 ticks (2.5 s)**: the magazine rocked out, the next rocked in, the bolt racked. **Draw 12 ticks (0.6 s)**.
- **Ammunition**: 30 in the rifle, up to 90 carried. Every third round is a tracer (seen in the air).
- **Held** — third person (GTA's): carried across him in both hands, the muzzle down, while he walks, runs and
  sprints; aimed (the other button held, or fired) its butt in his right shoulder, his chest a quarter turned, he
  faces the view and the keys strafe. First person (CS:GO's): fixed to the view at its lower right, the left hand on
  the fore-end, a cross in the middle.
